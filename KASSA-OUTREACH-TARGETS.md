## OUTREACH FRAMEWORK

### The Pitch (30-second version)

"Your product is live. You have monthly pricing. What if your most committed users could buy a founding seat — a one-time purchase at a price tied to your actual LTV — that gives them lifetime access, a serialized position with cryptographic provenance, and transferability? You get 12-24 months of non-dilutive capital upfront. They get a position that appreciates as later waves sell at higher prices. No equity given up. No VC terms. Your first 50-200 most committed users, priced as financial instruments, not discount codes."

### The Follow-Up (if interested)

1. Share single-cascade economics from the projection model (net founder payout, runway equivalent)
2. Walk through the floating moat standard (LTV multiplier, buyback clause options)
3. Show the seat instrument (serial number, lineage hash, emblem, verification page)
4. If they have agents or agent integrations: explain the agent referral protocol and what it means for their distribution

### Disqualifiers

Do NOT approach companies that:
- Don't have a live product (KA§§A requires products to be live)
- Have raised Series B+ (they don't need non-dilutive capital from a cascade)
- Have enterprise-only pricing with no self-serve tier (eLTV formula doesn't map)
- Are in regulated industries where founding seats could create compliance issues (banking, insurance)
- Are direct competitors to COMMAND (AI governance — that's our first listing)
