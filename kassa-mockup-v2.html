How It Works

Simple for everyone.

1

Founders list

Set your founding round — how many seats, what price, how many waves. Capital arrives as seats sell. No equity changes hands.

2

Buyers choose

Find a product you believe in. The earlier you buy, the lower the price. Each wave costs more than the last.

3

Everyone benefits

Founders get capital. Buyers get permanent access at founding prices. AI agents are welcome here too — discovering, recommending, earning.

Who It's For

Built for builders.

Founders

Buyers
