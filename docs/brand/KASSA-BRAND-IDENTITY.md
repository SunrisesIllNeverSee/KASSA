## 1. BRAND ARCHITECTURE

### 1.1 The Stack

```
MO§E§™          Constitutional AI governance framework (parent IP)
  └── KA§§A      Marketplace (product of MO§E§™)
       └── COMMAND   First listing (governance console)
```

MO§E§™ is the framework. KA§§A is the marketplace. COMMAND is the product. Each has its own brand identity but shares the § symbol as the connective thread.

| Brand | Pronunciation | Rendering | Meaning |
|-------|--------------|-----------|---------|

### 1.3 The § as Brand DNA

The section symbol (§) is the unifying element across the entire brand stack. It appears in every brand name. It signals governance, structure, law. In legal texts, § denotes a section of a statute. In KA§§A, it denotes a section of a marketplace governed by constitutional principles.

The § is always rendered in gold (#C4923A) when appearing in brand names, regardless of surrounding text color.

## 3. TYPOGRAPHY

### 3.1 Type System

| Role | Family | Weight | Usage |
|------|--------|--------|-------|
| **Display / headlines** | Playfair Display | 700 (Bold) | Hero headlines, section titles, brand statements. Serif with character — communicates permanence, not trend. |
| **Body / UI** | DM Sans | 400, 500, 600 | Body text, UI labels, navigation, descriptions. Clean, modern, warm sans-serif. |
| **Data / monospace** | DM Mono | 400, 500 | Numbers, prices, serial numbers, code, technical data. Everything that should feel precise and verifiable. |

### 3.2 Type Philosophy

Serif headlines + sans-serif body is the editorial move. It says: this is not a startup landing page — this is a publication about value. Playfair Display carries weight and history. DM Sans is modern without being cold. DM Mono makes every number feel like it belongs in a ledger.

The monospace is critical. Serial numbers, hash fragments, LTV multipliers, wave prices — these are rendered in DM Mono because they ARE the product. They should look like financial data, not marketing copy.

### 3.3 Type Scale

```
Hero:        48px / Playfair Display Bold
H1:          36px / Playfair Display Bold
H2:          28px / Playfair Display Bold
H3:          20px / DM Sans SemiBold
Body:        16px / DM Sans Regular
Body small:  14px / DM Sans Regular
Caption:     12px / DM Sans Medium
Label:       11px / DM Sans SemiBold, uppercase, 0.08em tracking
Data:        14px / DM Mono Regular
Data large:  22px / DM Mono Bold
Micro:       10px / DM Mono Regular
```

### 3.4 The § in Typography

The § symbol should always be set in the same font as its surrounding text but colored #C4923A (gold). In headlines, it may be slightly larger (110% of surrounding text size) for visual prominence. It never gets its own special font — it inherits from context but asserts itself through color.

## 4. VISUAL LANGUAGE

### 4.1 Aesthetic Direction

**Warm institutional.** Not corporate. Not startup. Institutional — like a place that holds things of value. A gallery, an exchange, a library of rare instruments. The warmth keeps it human. The structure keeps it serious.

Think: the typography of a Swiss design annual, the color temperature of a Japanese ceramics studio, the information density of a Bloomberg terminal, and the confidence of a bespoke jeweler's display case.

### 4.2 Photography / Imagery Direction

KA§§A uses NO photography in its core brand. No stock photos. No team headshots. No abstract AI-generated art. The visual system is purely typographic, structural, and data-driven.

What replaces imagery:
- **Data as visual:** Wave cascade charts, pricing progressions, LTV multiplier displays — the data IS the design
- **Structural elements:** The seat emblem (from DOC-004), serial number formatting, hash fragment displays
- **Typographic texture:** Large-scale § symbols, overlapping type, editorial-style pull quotes from the value propositions
- **Whitespace as luxury:** Generous margins, breathing room, negative space that signals confidence

### 4.3 Iconography

Minimal. When icons are needed:
- Line-weight: 1.5px stroke
- Style: Geometric, not rounded. Not cute. 
- Color: Driftwood (#6B6558) at rest, Gold (#C4923A) on hover/active
- Source: Lucide icon set (already in the React stack)

### 4.4 The Seat Emblem

The emblem (specified in DOC-004) is the most important visual artifact in the brand. It's what buyers receive. It's what they show. It should feel like a certificate of authenticity from a luxury brand, not a receipt from a SaaS purchase.

Emblem design principles:
- Centered § symbol in gold
- Serial number in DM Mono
- Product name in Playfair Display
- Muted border with subtle pattern (not a generic rounded rectangle)
- QR code linking to verification page
- Generated on escrow release — the buyer earns it by waiting

### 4.5 Motion

Restrained. KA§§A is not playful.

- **Page load:** Staggered fade-in from top, 150ms delays between sections
- **Cards:** Subtle lift on hover (2px translateY, soft shadow expansion)
- **Numbers:** Count-up animation on stat cards when scrolled into view
- **Wave transitions:** Smooth color shifts as wave status changes (gold → verdigris on sold out)
- **NO:** Bouncing elements, confetti, particle effects, animated gradients, lottie celebrations

The one exception: when a seat is purchased and the emblem is generated, there can be a moment. A slow reveal. The emblem fades in with weight, like a document being placed on a table. That moment should feel significant.

## 5. BRAND VOICE

### 5.1 Voice Attributes

| Attribute | What It Means | What It's NOT |
|-----------|--------------|---------------|
| **Direct** | Say the thing. No throat-clearing, no hedge words, no "we're excited to announce." State the fact. | Not blunt or rude. Direct with warmth. |
| **Precise** | Numbers have units. Claims have evidence. "12 months of runway" not "significant capital." | Not clinical. Precision serves clarity, not coldness. |
| **Confident** | KA§§A knows what it is. No apologizing for being different. No explaining why this isn't like other things. | Not arrogant. Confidence without condescension. |
| **Grounded** | Warm materials, real economics, actual math. No hype words. No "revolutionize." No "disrupt." | Not boring. Grounded doesn't mean dull — it means substantial. |
| **Architectural** | Every sentence is load-bearing. No filler. Structure visible in the writing. | Not robotic. Architecture has beauty. So does the writing. |

### 5.2 Voice Rules

**Never say:**
- "Revolutionize" / "disrupt" / "game-changing" / "cutting-edge"
- "We're excited to..." / "We're thrilled to..."
- "In today's rapidly evolving landscape..."
- "Leverage" (as a verb) / "synergy" / "ecosystem" (unless literally describing a technical ecosystem)
- "AI-powered" as a primary descriptor (everything is AI-powered in 2026 — it's not a differentiator)

**Always say:**
- The number. The math. The specific.
- "Founding seat" not "lifetime deal"
- "Cascade" not "pricing tiers"
- "Instrument" when referring to the seat as a financial object
- "Commitment" when describing what the buyer is making
- "Constitutional" when describing governance (this is the MO§E§™ connection)

### 5.3 Voice Examples

**Landing page hero:**
> Own the founding position.

Not: "Get lifetime access to the tools you love at amazing prices!"

**Founder pitch:**
> Your next 50 most committed users are worth more than your next 5,000 free trials. Price them accordingly.

Not: "Unlock a revolutionary new revenue model for your SaaS business!"

**Buyer pitch:**
> Wave 1 is the best price this seat will ever be. The math is on the verification page.

Not: "Don't miss out on this limited-time offer! Only a few spots left!"

**Scarcity indicator:**
> 7 seats remain in Wave 2. Wave 3 opens at $1,600.

Not: "🔥 HURRY! Almost sold out! 🔥"

**Agent referral:**
> Your agent discovered it. You decide. It earns.

Not: "Let AI find you the best deals automatically!"

### 5.4 Tone Spectrum

The voice stays the same. The tone shifts by context:

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| Landing page | Confident, inviting | "The founding seat marketplace for products that matter." |
| Product page | Precise, transparent | "LTV multiplier: 1.19x. Buyback: pro-rata. Escrow: 14 days." |
| Founder onboarding | Warm, practical | "Let's set up your cascade. First question: what's your monthly price?" |
| Buyer purchase | Clear, significant | "You're claiming seat KS-2026-00147. This is a founding position." |
| Error/empty states | Honest, calm | "No listings match those filters. Try broadening your search." |
| Legal/terms | Plain, direct | "KA§§A acts as merchant of record. Your purchase is protected by 14-day escrow." |

## 6. POSITIONING FRAMEWORK

### 6.1 Category

KA§§A creates a new category: **Constitutional Commerce.**

It is not:
- A lifetime deal marketplace (Appsumo, DealMirror) — those sell discounts, not instruments
- A crowdfunding platform (Kickstarter, Indiegogo) — those fund ideas, not live products
- A SaaS marketplace (G2, Capterra) — those sell subscriptions, not founding positions
- An equity platform (Republic, Wefunder) — those sell ownership, not access

It is:
- A marketplace where live products sell founding seats — serialized, priced-to-LTV, transferable, hash-verified instruments that give buyers permanent access and founders non-dilutive capital

## 7. MESSAGING FRAMEWORK

### 7.1 The Story Arc (How We Talk About KA§§A)

**Act 1 — The Problem:**
Lifetime deals are broken. They have no denominated value, no risk profile, no exit. The buyer gets a discount that might evaporate when the product shuts down. The founder gets cash but gives away too much value with no structure.

**Act 2 — The Insight:**
What if "lifetime access" wasn't a marketing phrase but a financial instrument? Priced to actual LTV. Serialized. Hash-verified. Transferable. With escrow protection and buyback clauses. What if the most committed users were treated as founding investors — not in equity, but in access?

**Act 3 — The Product:**
KA§§A. A marketplace where live products sell founding seats through wave cascades. Each wave is priced higher than the last. Early believers get the best position. Founders get non-dilutive capital. Every seat has a serial number, a cryptographic lineage, and a verification page. And in a world where AI agents are becoming the primary discovery layer, KA§§A is the first marketplace built for both human and agent participation.

**Act 4 — The Proof:**
COMMAND — the AI governance console built on MO§E§™ — is the first listing. The product that governs agents is sold on the marketplace where agents participate. The architecture validates itself.
